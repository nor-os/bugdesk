## Comments

### 2026-09-23T19:00Z · norman_agent (status: investigation -> testing)

A single click on a registry row now opens the product (`products_pane.js`, the delegated
`click` on the sheet). A click on the row's edit/delete icons, a header filter input, or a click
that ends a text selection does not count as a row click.

The row index now points into the rows ACTUALLY SHOWN, because the collections navigator (#11)
narrows the table. Indexing the unfiltered list would open the wrong product once a folder is
picked; `renderCell` and the row menu were moved onto the same array.

Verified: `governance/render.test.mjs` §1 asserts a margin-button click does not open, a cell click
does, and a click on a row in a narrowed table opens THAT product. Checked in headless Edge.
Uncommitted in the Tables working tree.
