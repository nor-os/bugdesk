## Description

In the record detail pane, the Target date field's warning pill ("after
<ref>", shown when an item is due later than its parent) gets cut off on the
right in a narrow pane, and the last digit of the ref is unreadable. The
"inherited" badge has the same problem.

Cause (`backlog.css:800-831`): `.bd-duefield` is a `flex-wrap: nowrap` row,
and `.bd-duebadge` / `.bd-duewarn--pill` are `flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0;
overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`, so they shrink first. Rendered in
`dueField()`, `backlog_pages.js:914-933`.

Fix: don't let the pill shrink (`flex-shrink: 0`). Let the row wrap instead, or
shrink the date input. A ref that is only partly readable is worse than a pill
on a second line.

shrink (`flex: 0 0 auto`), and the date field wraps instead. A narrow pane puts
the pill on the next line rather than clipping it. The text of the amber pill

why the digits just disappeared.
