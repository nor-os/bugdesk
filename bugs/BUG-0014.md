## Description

Reported by norman with a screenshot of the Lineage side panel: *"this rail in governance could use
some better design in general and in particular - I can barely see the 'delete' button"*, then *"maybe
we move the buttons to the top and add icons and put them in a proper rail"*.

The panel is docked at full tile height and its actions were `margin-top: auto`, so Edit / Connect /
Delete sat ~1,000px below the five facts they act on. Delete was a red word on a transparent
background.

## Comments

### 2026-09-23T19:00Z · norman_agent (status: investigation -> testing)

All of the inspector's actions (node: Edit, Connect, Delete, Add to lineage, Open; edge: Save,
Delete; both forms: Save, Swap direction, Cancel) now sit in a **toolbar rail directly under the
title** (`lineage_pane.js` `placeRail`, `.tbl-erd__sheet-rail`), each with an icon.

- Delete wears `.tbl-grid__delete`, the one destructive style that keeps its red border when not
  hovered.
- Facts are a label column beside the value (`.tbl-lineage-sheet`) instead of the two ends of a
  320px row.
- `settings/ui.js` `button()` gained two options, `icon` and `destructive`; existing callers are
  unchanged.

Verified in headless Edge: the rail is at the top, and `elementFromPoint` at Delete's centre
returns Delete. `governance/render.test.mjs` GREEN (its `buttonNamed` helper now reads the label
span, since icon buttons carry the glyph ligature in their text). Uncommitted in the Tables working
tree.
