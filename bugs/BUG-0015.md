## Description

Found while working on #13; not reported by anyone yet.

The product view's Overview is a live metadata form and its Contract section a live modeller; both
hold typed, unsaved state until their own Save. The tile renderer rebuilds a leaf when a tab is
opened beside it and unmounts the tab you leave, and `data-product` has no live registry (unlike
grids, `grid_registry.js`, and boards, `dashboard_registry.js`). So opening a tab beside a product,
or switching tabs, discards what you typed with no warning.

`web/js/shell/content.js`'s `dataProduct` docstring already records this as a debt ("NO REGISTRY,
AND THAT IS NOW A DEBT"). The fix is the registry treatment (a per-site live view, a token per
mount, deferred teardown, `isReferenced` over `leaf.tabs` and `leaf.pageTabs`) in `web/js/shell/`.

Repro: open a product, type in Overview without saving, open another product as a tab beside it,
switch back. The typed text is gone.
