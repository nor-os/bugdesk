## Description

What a Purview-class catalogue is expected to carry and Tables does not: a business glossary with
terms linked to products and columns; classifications and sensitivity labels at COLUMN level (today
a classification is per product); data-quality indicators; and usage statistics (who reads a
product, how often). None of this is designed in `docs/`, so this story starts with a decision
about which of these are wanted.
