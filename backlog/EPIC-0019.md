## Description

Configure how a project's stores behave, instead of relying on fixed
conventions: how records are numbered (STORY-0002), whether and when BugDesk
saves, commits and pushes (STORY-0006, STORY-0020), and moving a record from
one store to the other (STORY-0001). Every setting defaults to today's
behaviour and is stored in the committed `.bugdesk/project.json`.
