## Description

The Tables governance section (data products, source consent, collections, lineage, source
systems, change log) is built and proved against the running stack. This work package collects
what it still does NOT do, as of 2026-09-23, so the gaps are visible and can be ordered rather than
rediscovered.

Sources: `Tables/docs/21-data-products.md` (§ The four properties, § Contracts, § Sharing beyond
the deployment) and `Tables/docs/23-implementation-status.md` (§ What is not built). Items 2–4 and
10–11 are recorded there; 7–9 are catalogue expectations (Purview-class) that no document has
decided on yet.

Suggested order if nothing else decides it: STORY-0003 (the DDL edge, the largest real gap), then
STORY-0002 (access requests), then STORY-0004 (reminders), which unblocks STORY-0005.
